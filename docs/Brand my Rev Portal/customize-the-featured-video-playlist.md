Access Rev's Branding options through the **Admin > System Settings > Branding** menu option.

The Featured Video playlist in Rev uses the Featured Video template on [The Rev Home Page](doc:the-rev-home-page).  You can use the **Featured Video** tab under the **Branding **menu to change the default Poster Carousel template that is in use originally . Depending on the template you use, different options are available for its further customization.

## Choosing the Featured Content for the Featured Video Carousel

No matter which template you select for your **Featured Video** carousel widget, you have different content options you can choose to populate it using the Featured Content dropdown.

- **Featured Video Playlist** - This is the default option and allows you to have exact control over what is presented in the carousel based on what videos you add to the [Featured Video playlist](doc:edit-the-featured-video-playlist).
- **Most Popular (7 days)** - This selection loads the most popular 10 videos (based on number of views) in the portal over the last seven days.
- **Recommended for You** - This selection loads the 10 videos (based on the specific user logged into the portal) that is related to their viewing habits.  Note that this will change over time and also be different based on the user.

There are two selections that must be made when you choose the Classic template in addition to selecting the [Featured Content](doc:customize-the-featured-video-playlist#choosing-the-featured-content-for-the-featured-video-carousel):

- Banner Background (1600 x 1200 pixels, not larger than 2MB recommended)
- Auto Scroll (on or off)

The **Banner Background** for the Classic template may be a color or an image. The default color is #141414 (black). If you select **Image **,  you must use an image that is at least 1600 pixels wide by 1200 pixels high (and no larger than 2 MB). The image selected only appears across the Featured Video background on the Home Page.

If you elect to turn **Auto Scroll** on, you must enter a number between 1 and 10. This determines the number of seconds each video in the Featured Playlist is on the screen before it auto-scrolls to the next video in the playlist. If auto scroll is off, the user must manually click the next arrow to view the next video in the playlist.

## Featured Video Poster Carousel Template

The default Featured Video template on the Home Page is the **Poster Carousel **template until it is changed. This template features a full-screen “poster” version of each video’s thumbnail as the background along with the video's **Title **and a **Watch Now** button.

## Featured Video Tiles Template

There are no additional settings that must be made when you choose the **Tiles **template for the Featured Video playlist other than selecting the [Featured Content](doc:customize-the-featured-video-playlist#choosing-the-featured-content-for-the-featured-video-carousel). 

If you select **Featured Video** playlist as the Featured Content, you may modify what appears here by [editing the order](doc:playlists#edit-the-order-of-the-videos-in-a-playlist) the playlist itself. If more than 5 videos are in the playlist, only the first 5 are used.
