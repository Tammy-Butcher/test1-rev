Access Rev's Branding options through the **Admin > System Settings > Branding** menu option.

The Featured Video playlist in Rev uses the Featured Video template on [The Rev Home Page](doc:the-rev-home-page).  You can use the **Featured Video** tab under the **Branding** menu to change the default template and content settings in use to choose your own. Depending on the template you use, different options are available for its further customization which are described below.

<Image title="featuredVideoTemplates.png" alt="Use the Featured Video tab to customize the Featured Video template and Featured Content that will appear on the Home Page" align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/2fd60220475d5de318275516bbb7f28edee2ff707761289920e852905641a544-featuredVideoTab.png">

</Image>

## Choosing Featured Content

No matter which template you select for your **Featured Video** widget, you have different content options you can then choose to populate it with using the **Featured Content** dropdown. The options include:

* **Featured Playlist** - This is the default option and loads only those videos that are part of the [Featured Video playlist](doc:edit-the-featured-video-playlist).
* **Featured Events** - This selection loads only events that have been specified as [Featured Events](doc:create-a-featured-event). Keep in mind that once the event has concluded, it is no longer displayed in the widget so only those organizations that are very event heavy should consider this settings.
* **Featured Events + Playlist** - This selection combines the two above settings so that you get both the Featured Playlist and Featured Events appearing in your widget. If you select this combination, you can also specify if you want videos to display in the Featured Playlist before Featured Events are displayed.
* **Most Popular (7 days)** - This selection loads the most popular 10 videos (based on number of views) in the portal over the last seven days.
* **Recommended for You** - This selection loads the 10 videos (based on the specific user logged into the portal) that is related to their viewing habits.  Note that this will change over time and also be different based on the user.

## Featured Video Classic Template

 This template features a traditional "boxed" version of each video with a play button in the center of the video.

<Image title="classicFVPreview.png" alt="A preview of the Classic Featured Video template with an image banner background" align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/e5bd5053416ccb72a7c06889de9512e3468ae075b6cbf111509d274872476c44-featuredVidClassicTemp.png">
  A preview of the Classic Featured Video template with an image banner background
</Image>

The additional selections that must be made when you choose the **Classic** carousel template in addition to selecting the content are:

* **Banner Background** (1600 x 1200 pixels, not larger than 2MB recommended) or a color
* **Auto Scroll** (on or off) and the **Auto Scroll Delay** (in seconds) if enabled

<Image title="classicFVOptions.png" alt="Select a Banner Background option and whether or not to Auto Scroll the videos in the Classic Featured Video template" align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/b2053c099daadfb70d08c3580006f22a7944b34c5dfc07afc4c231dfe486fb8c-carouselClassicTemplate.png">
  Select a Banner Background option and whether or not to Auto Scroll the videos in the Classic Featured Video template
</Image>

The **Banner Background** may be a color or an image. The default color is #141414 (black). If you select **Image** ,  you must use an image that is at least 1600 pixels wide by 1200 pixels high (and no larger than 2 MB). The image selected only appears across the Featured Video background on the Home Page.

If you elect to turn **Auto Scroll** on, you must enter a number between 1 and 10. This determines the number of seconds each video in the Featured Playlist is on the screen before it auto-scrolls to the next video in the playlist. If auto scroll is off, the user must manually click the next arrow to view the next video in the playlist.

As noted above, you must selected the [Featured Content](doc:customize-the-featured-video-playlist#choosing-featured-content) that appears in the dropdown.

## Featured Video Poster Carousel Template

The default widget on the Home Page is the **Poster Carousel** template until it is changed. This template features a full-screen “poster” version of each video’s thumbnail as the background along with the video's **Title** and a **Go to Video** button. 

<Image title="posterFVPreview.png" alt="A preview of the Poster Carousel Featured Video template" align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/661d751c203a41cd8b3e261721ec2d7d13c8d6acc9f496704ac8dff9bbd81097-featuredVidPoster.png">
  A preview of the Poster Carousel Featured Video template
</Image>

The additional selections that must be made when you choose the **Poster Carousel** template in addition to selecting the content are:

* **Text Alignment** settings where your text can be left, center, and right aligned over your content
* Choose to enable or disable the **Show Full Overlay** setting on your content.  If you enable an overlay, this replaces the default gradient that Rev uses with a solid color that you choose. The **Overlay Color** is a Hex value that can have its **Opacity** adjusted in increments of 10 from 0 to 100%.
* You can remove the overlay by placing a 0 in the **Opacity** setting.
* If you disable the overlay and keep the default gradient, this cannot be customized.

You can choose **Text Alignment** options on this template (left, center, and right) as well as enable an **Overlay Color**, including **Opacity** effects.

<Image alt="When using the Poster Carousel template, you can decide how your Title text will align and use Overlay features" align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/94307540555531c410df940a452a2fe0e9a47052c160bd9d564455a6ccc9d9d0-posterTemplateCarouselSettings.png">
  When using the Poster Carousel template, you can decide how your Title text will align and use Overlay features
</Image>

Just as with the Classic template option, you must decide on **Auto Scroll** selections and choose the [Featured Content](doc:customize-the-featured-video-playlist#choosing-featured-content) that appears in the dropdown.

> 🚧 Important!
>
> Be aware that it is possible to configure font, thumbnail, and opacity settings here so that accessibility is affected.  Proceed with caution and double-check your settings when using these features.

## Featured Video Tiles Template

There are no additional settings that must be made when you choose the **Tiles** template for the **Featured Video** playlist other than selecting the [Featured Content](doc:customize-the-featured-video-playlist#choosing-featured-content). 

If you select **Featured Video** playlist as the content, you may modify what appears here by [editing the order](doc:playlists#edit-the-order-of-the-videos-in-a-playlist) the playlist itself. If more than 5 videos are in the playlist, only the first 5 are used.
