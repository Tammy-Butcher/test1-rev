Access Rev's Branding options through the **Admin > System Settings > Branding** menu option.

Use the **Header **tab under the **Branding **menu to change the Rev **Header **  look and feel on your portal. You can modify the colors and change the template from the **Classic **template to a **Two Tier** or **Modern **template depending on your organization's own branding and style usage. 

> 🚧 Important!
> 
> Remember, there are specific recommended [logo sizes](doc:fonts-colors-and-logo#upload-a-custom-logo) to use based on the **Header **template you select if you are also customizing your logo.

## Customize Header Colors

Use the **Header Color** section to modify the **Header Background Color** and **Header Font Color**.

- Click the color picker next to the color you want to modify. You may also enter a hexadecimal value. The colors applied here \_only \_apply to the Header of the portal.
- Click the **Full Size Preview** button to preview your changes.
- Click the **Reset Default** button to revert back to the default colors of the Header

> 👍 Tip
> 
> [Default colors](doc:fonts-colors-and-logo#reset-primary-and-accent-colors) on the **General** tab also apply to parts of the portal Header and Rev's [login flow](doc:your-rev-account) pages to ensure logo contrast. Keep this in mind if you modify them.

## Choose a Header Template

Use the **Header Template** section to modify the Header’s style and UI. The **Classic **template is the default style.

Each style is previewed next to its template. However, you may also click the **Full Size Preview** button to preview the template before clicking the **Save **button and applying your changes.

### Two-Tier Header Template

This header is a two-tiered or layered header that splits the menu options into a separate layer from the logo, searches, uploads, and notification icon layer. When this template is used, the next **Upcoming Event** is also displayed in the center of the header.
