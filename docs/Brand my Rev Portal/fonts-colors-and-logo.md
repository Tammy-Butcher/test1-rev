Access Rev's Branding options through the **Admin > System Settings > Branding** menu option.

Use the **General** tab under the **Branding** menu to customize the Rev portal logo, colors, and font.

## Upload a Custom Logo

A custom logo replaces the Vbrick Rev logo in the upper left corner of the portal interface so that you may brand your Rev portal with your own logo and colors. If you change the logo, Rev system email templates are also modified and branded with the customized logo you use. Otherwise, the default Rev logo is used.

<Image title="customLogo.png" alt={352} align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/a9abe6f-customLogo.png">
  Upload your own logo to replace the Vbrick Rev logo
</Image>

* Click the **Choose File** button to upload an image. The [image](doc:supported-file-types) may be in .jpg, .png, or .gif format
* You may upload a logo of any size, however, there are recommended sizes based on the [Header template](doc:customize-the-portal-header) you use. Specifically:
  * **Classic** - 50 pixel maximum height
  * **Two Tier** - 75 pixel maximum height
  * **Modern** - 75 pixel maximum height
* Click the **Full Size Preview** button if you want to preview your logo before saving it
* Click the **Reset Default** button to return to the Rev logo (only appears when you upload a new logo)

## Primary and Accent Colors

Use the **Colors** section to specify **Primary** and **Accent** colors for portal components and fonts. The default color of each section is demonstrated in the image below.

<Image alt="The Rev portal default colors" align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/6a42c62-defaultColors.png">
  The Rev portal default colors
</Image>

Modify the default colors by clicking the color picker or entering a hexadecimal value in the color field. Use the Reset Default link to reset any customizations you have made.  Be aware that the colors you choose do [apply branding and theme updates](doc:apply-ui-branding-and-theme-colors) throughout the portal.

> 👍 Tip
>
> Default colors also apply to parts of the portal [Header](doc:customize-the-portal-header) you use and Rev's [login flow](doc:your-rev-account) pages to ensure logo contrast.  Keep this in mind if you modify them.

## Upload a Custom Font

Rev’s default font for the portal is Roboto. You may modify this and upload your own fonts to use so long as it has the **Web Open Font Format (.woff)** extension.

<Image title="customFont.png" alt={352} align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/99f25f3-customFont.png">
  Use any custom font of your choice with the portal so long as it has the .woff extension
</Image>

* Click the **Choose File** button to upload a new font
* Click the **Full Size Preview** button if you want to preview your font change before saving it
* Click the **Reset Default** button to return to the default font (only appears when you upload a new font)
