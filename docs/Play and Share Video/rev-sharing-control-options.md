Rev Sharing Controls are available when you use features such as [Screen Share and Record](doc:screen-share-and-record), [Producer Events](doc:produce-an-event), and [Presenting a Webcam and Screenshare Event](doc:host-a-self-produced-webcast).

### Sharing Your Screen

Clicking the **Share Screen** button prompts you to select what screen(s) you are going to share.  Note that you can select from your **Entire Screen**, a specific **Window**, or a browser **Tab** depending on what you currently have open and what you have configured as part of your general system setup.  

You can also click the **Share system audio** checkbox to share what audio may be currently playing (if you have audio as part of a presentation for example).

<Image title="shareScreenSelfProduce.png" alt={756} align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/3b0e975-shareScreenSelfProduce.png">
  Once you start an event, decide what you are going to share as part of it
</Image>

Keep in mind that you can always change what you are sharing or even change the screen you are sharing by clicking the **Change Screen** button (once you have started sharing a screen).  The **Stop Share** button will stop sharing the screen completely if needed.

### Sharing Your Camera and Microphone

Use the **Start Camera** and **Start Microphone** buttons to use the default webcam and microphone options available on your computer.  These can always be changed if you have more than one option available.

<Image alt="The Start Camera and Start Microphone buttons use the default webcam and microphone you have on your system" align="center" width="50% " src="https://files.readme.io/7042c6c-webcamScreenShareButtons.png">
  The Start Camera and Start Microphone buttons use the default webcam and microphone you have on your system
</Image>

### Rev Producer AV Controls

Rev Producer has slightly different camera and microphone controls than a Webcam and Screenshare event but the functionality is, essentially, the same.

| Sharing Icons                                                   | Usage                                                                                                                                                        |
| :-------------------------------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| ![](https://files.readme.io/9353ea0-smallAVControlsOff.png)     | Sharing icons are **red** and have a line through them if they are **not** in use.                                                                           |
| ![](https://files.readme.io/a11364d-smallAVControlsOn.png)      | Sharing icons are **green** with no line through them when they **are** in use.                                                                              |
| ![](https://files.readme.io/59502c6-smallAVControlsOptions.png) | To better support accessibility, if more than one option is available, a flyout appears to the right of each selection if more than one option is available. |
