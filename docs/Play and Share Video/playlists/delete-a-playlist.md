Use the **My Playlists** interface to delete an entire playlist. Both **Static** and **Dynamic** playlists can be deleted.

1. Navigate to the **Media** > [My Playlists](doc:user-menu-options).

2. The **My Playlists** option displays the **Grid** view by default.  Only the **List** view can be used to delete a playlist.

3. Click on the checkbox next to the playlist you want to delete in **List** view.

> 🚧 Important!
>
> You may *not* delete the **Featured Videos** playlist. This is a built-in Vbrick Rev feature.

4. Click the **Trash** icon and the selected playlist is deleted. *Only* the playlist is deleted; not the videos that are part of the playlist.
