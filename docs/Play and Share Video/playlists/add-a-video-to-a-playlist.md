The method you use to add videos to a playlist depends on if the playlist is **Static** or **Dynamic**.

## Add Videos to a Static Playlist

Static playlists are manually updated so the best method to add to to them is from the video itself using the **Add to Playlist** icon.

<Image alt="Add to Playlist" align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/1428322-playlistIcon.png">
  Add to Playlist
</Image>

To add a video to a **Static** playlist(s):

1. Navigate to the video you want to add and open the [Playlists](doc:rev-video-player-features#video-playlists) flyout using the **Add to Playlist** icon.  

2. Click the checkbox next to the playlist you want to add the video to.  Note that you may add it to more than one playlist at a time as seen in the image below. The number next to the playlist name indicates how many videos are already part of the playlist and **Date Modified** also indicates the last time a video was added.

3. Click the **Save** button to add the video.

<Image alt=" You may add a video to more than one playlist at a time." align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/a5e5e85-addVidStatic.png">
   You may add a video to more than one playlist at a time.
</Image>

> 👍 Tip
>
> Checking a playlist for a video and then clicking **Save** adds the video to that playlist while unchecking it and then clicking **Save** will remove the video from the playlist.

4. You also have the option to click the **New** button on the **Playlist** flyout and add the video directly to a new playlist from here.  However, keep in mind this will *always* be a **Static** playlist.  To create a [Dynamic playlist type](doc:playlists), you can do so from the **Media > My Playlists** menu option.

<Image alt="Clicking the New button will create a new Static playlist" align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/ae18814-newStaticFromTab.png">
  Clicking the New button will create a new Static playlist
</Image>

## Add Videos to a Dynamic Playlist

Dynamic playlists are updated automatically based on filter criteria you select.

To add a video to a **Dynamic** playlist(s):

1. Navigate to to **Media > My Playlists**. Click the playlist you want to add videos to and then click the **Edit Playlist** button to access the filter function.

> 👍 Tip
>
> If you are creating the Dynamic playlist for the first time, the filter function opens automatically for you.  Otherwise, you need to click the Edit Playlist button to edit filters.

2. Use Rev's [Filter Functions](doc:search-and-filter-functions) to add videos to the playlist.
3. In the example below, videos with the following filters are added automatically to this "Language Series" training Dynamic playlist in the future:
   1. "Speaking" is in the Title
   2. Added to the Language Series Category
   3. Added to the New Releases Category
   4. Video is in Active Status
