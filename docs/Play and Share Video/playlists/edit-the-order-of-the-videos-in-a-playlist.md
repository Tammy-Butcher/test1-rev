Use the **My Playlists** interface to reorder the videos in a **Static** playlist.

1. Navigate to **Media** > [My Playlists](doc:user-menu-options).

2. Click on the **Static** playlist you want to edit. Videos are listed in the order they are added with the most recent displayed first.  The order can be changed in **list** view.

3. Click and drag videos to the desired play order of the playlist.

4. Your changes are automatically saved when your drop a video into place.

<Image align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/10b9dc3-reorderVideos.png" />

> 🚧 Important!
>
> You can only edit the order of a **Dynamic** playlist by changing the **Sort** order since these are automatically curated by Rev.
