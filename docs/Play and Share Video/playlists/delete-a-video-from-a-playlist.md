There are two methods to removing a video from a **Static** Playlist.

**Use the Playlists flyout on the video**

1. Navigate to the video and access the [Playlists](doc:rev-video-player-features#video-playlists) flyout.

2. Each playlist the video belongs to has a checkmark next to it.  Uncheck the playlist you want to remove the video from and click the **Save** button.

3. The video will no longer be a part of that playlist.

**Use the My Playlists option**

1. Navigate to the **Media** > [My Playlists](doc:user-menu-options).

2. Click on the playlist you want to edit.

3. Click the checkbox next to the video you want to delete and then click the **Trash** icon to the right.

4. The video will no longer be part of that playlist.
