The **Featured Video** playlist appears on the [Home Page](doc:the-rev-home-page) and is a good method for displaying important information to your organization. This playlist is automatically created and packaged with Rev and you add videos to it exactly as you would one of your own custom created playlists.

To view the the **Featured Videos** playlist:

1. Select the **Media** > **My Playlists** option in the [User account navigation menu](doc:user-menu-options).

2. Select the **Featured Videos** tile. The number under the tile indicates how many videos are already part of the playlist.

3. Edit and manage the playlist as you would an individual playlist including [adding new videos](doc:add-a-video-to-a-playlist), [editing the order of videos](doc:edit-the-order-of-the-videos-in-a-playlist), or [removing videos from the playlist](doc:delete-a-video-from-a-playlist).

4. You can also [share and embed the Featured Playlist](doc:share-a-playlist) and apply layout and styling effects to it.

> 📘 Note
> 
> Only Account and Media Admins may andd and update the Featured Videos playlist.  All other accounts may only share and embed the Featured Videos playlist.
