There are two different methods you can use in Rev to share your **Static** or **Dynamic** playlists with other viewers; linking to it and embedding it in third-party players. Use the **My Playlists** interface to link or embed a playlist so that other users can view it using your chosen method.

1. Navigate to the **Media **> [My Playlists](doc:user-menu-options).

2. Click on the playlist you want to share or embed.  Click the **Sharing **icon to the right of the playlist.

3. The **Sharing **options dialogue box appears with the **Link **tab selected by default.  Click the **Embed **tab to view those options if desired.   Each method is described below.

## Link a Playlist

When you use the **Link** tab to link to a playlist, users are prompted to log-in to the Rev portal to view the playlist if the videos are not public. The **Link** tab is selected by default when you open the [Playlists](doc:rev-video-player-features#video-playlists) flyout.

To link to a playlist:

1. Navigate to the **Media **> [My Playlists](doc:user-menu-options) dropdown and click on a playlist you want to share.
2. Click the **Playlist** sharing icon to open the [Playlists](doc:rev-video-player-features#video-playlists) flyout.
3. Select the **Link** tab if it is not already selected by default.
4. Click the **Copy** link to copy the playlist link to the browser clipboard. Not all browsers support this function. The Copy button is not present if your browser does not support clipboard copy. You may share the link as needed.
5. Click the **Email** icon to open your default email program with the playlist inside the email body. The playlist title is also included. Not all browsers support this function. You may need to manually copy and paste your email into your email program of choice.
6. Email recipients are prompted to log-in to Rev if the videos in the playlist are not public.

## Embed a Playlist

To embed a playlist:

1. Navigate to the **Media **> [My Playlists](doc:user-menu-options) dropdown and click on a playlist you want to embed.
2. Click the **Playlist** sharing icon to open the [Playlists](doc:rev-video-player-features#video-playlists) flyout.
3. Select the **Embed** tab if it is not already selected. Use the **Embed Options** section to select **Layout** and **Playback** options for your playlist embed.
4. Click the **Copy **button to copy the playlist embed code that is present once you have finished styling it. The **Copy** button is not present if your browser does not support clipboard copy.  Click the **Reset** button to return all styling options you make to their default options.

### Layout Embed Options

The **Layout** options area includes the ability to modify the playlist **Size** and **Layout**.

**Size**: The default size selection is **Responsive**.  You can also change this dropdown to **Custom** and set a specific **Width** and **Height**.

**Layout**: You can choose one of three layouts for your playlist; **Row**, **Grid**, or **Slider**.  **Row** is the default.

- **Filmstrip** - Appears with a large player on top and features a horizontal filmstrip beneath it. The duration of each video is displayed in the lower right of each thumbnail along with its title.  The currently playing video is highlighted.  Left and right arrow keys are on each end of the row. Styling color options are not available for this option.
- **Grid**: Allows you to embed a grid of videos by specifying the **maximum number of videos per row** and the **maximum number of videos in the grid**. **Styling** options can be applied with primary color applied to the grid background, primary font color applied to the titles, and accent color applied to the player menu accent color.
- **Slider**: The first video in the playlist is loaded with left and right arrows in the lower right corner of the player for scrolling.  The slider layout accepts all color styling options (see Styling embed options below).

When you make a layout selection, the preview window displays to the left that displays how your embedded playlist will appear using the settings you select before you embed it.  The preview window works in real-time which means that if you change a setting, such as the **Layout** size or type, the preview window automatically updates. You can always click **Reset** to return to the default options.

### Styling Embed Options

Use the **Styling** options area to modify the **Accent Color**, **Primary Color**, and **Primary Font Color** of the embedded playlist.  Color options are applied depending upon the **Layout** option you select as described above. Just as with layout options, your preview window will update in real-time as you make your selections.

### Playback Embed Options

Use the **Playback** options area to set the **Autoplay** toggle for the playlist.
