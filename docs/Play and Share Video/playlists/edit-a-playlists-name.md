Use the **My Playlists** menu option to rename your **Static** playlists.

1. Navigate to the **Media **> [My Playlists](doc:user-menu-options).

2. Click on the **Edit **link next to the playlist name at the top of the screen.  Edit and save the new name.

> 🚧 Important!
> 
> You may _not_ rename the **Featured Videos** playlist. This is a built-in Vbrick Rev feature.

3. A **Dynamic** playlist is automatically maintained by Rev so you may not rename it once it is created.  If you want a new name, you should recreate the [Dynamic playlist](doc:create-a-new-playlist) with its new name and then [delete the old playlist](doc:delete-a-playlist) instead.
