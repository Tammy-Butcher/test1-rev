There are two types of **Playlists** that you can create in Rev through the **Menu > My Playlist** option; **Static** and **Dynamic**. 

Videos can also be added to a **Static** [Playlist](doc:rev-video-player-features#video-playlists) by clicking the **Playlist** icon.

- Playlists are _not _ unique in the system
- Playlist names _can _ contain numbers, letters, and special characters
- Playlists are _only _ visible to the user that creates them unless they are shared
- Playlists are _not _ visible to Admins

Each playlist type has its benefits.  Here are some reasons why you might use one over the other.

## Static Playlists

**Static playlists** are good to use when you have a specific theme or experience in mind because they are manually curated.  They do not change unless you edit them, including the order of the videos you add. You handpick each video that is included so Static Playlists are good for content that does not change frequently. A good use case for a Static playlist might be training or onboarding videos where certain content must be used every time and in a specific order.

## Dynamic Playlists

Unlike Static playlists, a **Dynamic playlist** is generated automatically by Rev based on filters you create. This means that content might change frequently, including the order of the videos, and it is updated automatically as well with no intervention needed from you. A Dynamic playlist is good for keeping content fresh and relevant or updating evolving interests. A good use case for a Dynamic playlist might be a new release playlist where new feature videos are added automatically for each new update of a product.
