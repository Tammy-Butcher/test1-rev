Webcast layout controls dictate where video, audio, and presentation slides appear during the event. Where these controls appear also depends upon your role and where the webcast is sourced from.

## Webcast Attendee Controls

**Attendees **toggle how the webcast layout appears (and functions) by using the tabs at the **top **of the webcast window.

The **Video**, **Audio**, and **Presentation **tabs are used to toggle those content elements of the webcast on and off. The elements are “on” by default when the webcast is started. If a tab is clicked, that element is toggled off until the tab is clicked again to turn it back on.

For example, if the **Presentation **tab is clicked, the large presentation window is removed until it is clicked again to turn the presentation back on.

The **Toggle Layout** tab orients the video and presentation interfaces between vertical and horizontal.

The same elements are available (in icon format) on mobile interfaces.

## Enhanced Slider Webcast Controls

Webcasts that are VC-sourced (such as a **Zoom **video conference or **Webex Meeting** streamed events) have an enhanced slider control that attendees may use for finer grained control over the layout and sizing of the Speaker’s video and content during playback. 

**View**: [Video Conference (VC) Integrations](doc:video-conference-vc-integrations) for details on these types of webcasts.

These controls appear in the Player bar at the bottom right of the webcast window.
