The **Video Source** section in event settings determines where your webcast receives video from to broadcast to your attendees.  

Think of Rev presentation profiles as "device profiles" that are comprised of more traditional video sources while other tabs that appear here depend on what integrations are enabled with Rev.  You can also use RTMP streams and even a previously recorded video as a source for your webcast. If you are not sure which source to use, ask your Account Admin. 

This section also dictates "how" your event begins such as automatically at the designated start time and whether or not it is automated or under your manual control.
