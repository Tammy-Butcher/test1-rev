Event Hosts and Event Moderators may display and manage all polls that have been created once broadcasting begins. Polls may also be edited or created after the event begins without the need to leave the webcast. [Polls must be enabled](doc:attendee-engagement#enable-polls-for-an-event) during event set up before this functionality may be used.

<Image title="openPoll.png" alt={432} align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/f62445b-openPoll.png">
  The Polls icon is used to view all polls that have been set up for the webcast
</Image>

## Display a Poll to Attendees

1. Click the **Polls** icon to display all polls entered during the event setup.

2. Use the right and left arrows to page through and select the poll you want to display to attendees.

3. You can also click the dots at the top of the poll interface to select different polls.  The number of polls available is indicated in the upper right corner.

4. Click the icons in the upper right corner to choose how you want to display the poll; bar or donut.  Attendees also have the option to choose how they display poll answers.
   * When a **bar chart** (default selection), the number displayed to the right of the bar is the percentage of responses selected for that item.
   * When a **donut** is selected, each color slice represents the percentage selected for that response.  Note that this selection is only available for **single answer** polls.  Polls that allow multiple responses may not use this option.

<Image title="donutPoll.png" alt={434} align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/f1c9fa5-donutPoll.png">
  Clicking the donut icon displays a donut poll to attendees when you Close the poll
</Image>

5. Click the **Open Poll** button to display the poll to attendees.  They will be able to answer it at that time.  Poll results are updated as attendees respond (and if they change their results) until you close the poll.  This button toggles to **Close Poll**.

## Edit a Poll

If you have not opened a poll (i.e., it is closed) and it contains no results, you can edit it during a webcast.  Click the **Edit** button on the poll to make edits.

<Image title="editPoll.png" alt={431} align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/5abf725-editPoll.png">
  Click the Edit button on a closed poll that has no results
</Image>

You may edit the poll question, its answers, and whether or not multiple answers are allowed on it by attendees.  Click the **Save** button to save your edits or **Cancel** to return to the original version of the poll.

## Close a Poll and Publish Results to Attendees

Use the **Close Poll** button to close an open poll so that results may be published to attendees. Only polls that have been closed may have results published.  The button changes to **Open Poll** after the poll is closed.

<Image title="publishPollResults.png" alt={435} align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/d4c155c-publishPollResults.png">
  The Publish Results button is only available on closed polls. This button displays poll results to attendees.
</Image>

Click the **Publish Results** button to display the results of the poll to attendees. The **Hide Results** button appears once they are published. You must hide the results of the poll before you may open it again.

> 👍 Tip
>
> The **Publish Results** button is not visible until you close the poll.

The **Total Responses** received for the entire poll as well as the responses received for each individual answer are displayed for attendees.

## Add a Poll During a Webcast

Create a poll on the fly during a webcast broadcast by clicking the **Create Poll** button.  The interface and functionality is exactly the same when [adding a poll](doc:attendee-engagement#enable-polls-for-an-event) during event set up.
