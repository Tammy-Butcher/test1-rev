The creator of the event is specified as the **Uploader** by default in the **Webcast Recording** section but it may be changed to any system user during set up. Further, it is *not* limited to the Hosts or Moderators of the event.

At least one uploader is required but no more than *one* may be selected.  It may *not* be edited during the webcast.
