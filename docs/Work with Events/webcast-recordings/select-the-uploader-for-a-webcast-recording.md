The creator of the event is specified as the **Uploader ** by default in the **Webcast Recording** section but it may be changed to any system user during set up. Further, it is _not _ limited to the Hosts or Moderators of the event.

At least one uploader is required but no more than _one_ may be selected.  It may _not_ be edited during the webcast.
