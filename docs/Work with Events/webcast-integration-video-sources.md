Rev integrates with a variety of video sources that you may use to power your events. An overview of each is provided here.  Note that each one requires that the integration is installed and configured *before* the tabs are visible and available as video sources in your Rev events.  You may need assistance from your Account Admin.  For complete details on a specific integration, view our [Rev Integration Guides](doc:rev-integration-guides).

## Video Conference (VC) Live Events

You can use Rev cloud to broadcast your video conferencing software so that you take advantage of your video conference while using Rev’s Webcast features at the same time. You need a **SIP** address to stream a video conference to your Rev event and to use this video source. 

**View**: [Schedule a Video Conference Webcast](doc:video-conference-vc-integrations#schedule-a-video-conference-webcast) for details on how to run a VC webcast once the integration is configured.

## Microsoft Teams

This will stream a Microsoft Teams video conference to your Rev meeting.  You can use a **Microsoft Teams Meeting** as a video source for your webcast event with both Microsoft Teams integration settings enabled. You are prompted to enter a Microsoft Teams Meeting URL. This means you can save the recording once it is finished and use Rev’s video settings and metadata features. 

**View**: [Microsoft Teams](doc:microsoft-teams#schedule-a-ms-teams-webcast-in-rev) for details on usage.

## Webex Team Events

You can use **Webex Teams** as a source for your Webcast event during event setup. When you select Webex Teams as a source, you may search from a list of teams that you belong to and you are not be required to enter a SIP address. This option streams what is on Rev's portal window to the Webex Team window. 

**View**: [Stream a Scheduled Rev Webcast Event to a Webex Team](doc:webex-teams#stream-a-scheduled-rev-webcast-event-to-a-webex-team) for details.

## Webex Meeting Events

As a Webex meeting Host, you can use Webex Meetings Live Streaming feature and have it serve as the video source for a new Rev Webcast event or a previously scheduled Rev Webcast event.  This option streams a Webex Meeting video conference to a Rev Meeting either on-the-fly or to one that is previously scheduled. 

**View**: [Webex Meetings](doc:webex-meetings#stream-a-webex-meeting-to-a-new-or-scheduled-rev-webcast-event) for configuration details.

## Zoom Meetings

This option streams a **Zoom** video conference to your Rev webcast.  You can also record a Zoom meeting and save it as a Rev VOD.  These features are available if you have enabled the **Zoom meeting Integration**.

**View**: [Zoom](doc:zoom-integration) integration configuration and usage

## Pexip Meetings

This option streams a **Pexip** video conference to your Rev webcast.  You can also record a Pexip meeting and save it as a Rev VOD.  These features are available if you have enabled the **Pexip meeting Integration**.

**View**: [Pexip](doc:pexip) integration configuration and usage
