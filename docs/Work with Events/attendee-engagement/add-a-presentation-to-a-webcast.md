You are easily able to display and control a PowerPoint presentation in addition to your streaming video for your webcast event.

> ❗️ Caution!
>
> If your PowerPoint contains non-standard fonts, you must embed them for proper display. Select **File** > **Options** then the **Embed fonts** in file checkbox.

<Image title="presentationFile.png" alt={915} src="https://files.readme.io/141574e-presentationFile.png">
  Select a presentation to display along with your video for the event
</Image>

* Click the **Choose a File** button to upload a presentation to the event
* A [supported file type](doc:supported-file-types)  must be used or it will not display properly
* When a presentation is uploaded, the **Choose a File** button is replaced by the **Replace File** button
* Select the **Enable Download** checkbox if you want your attendees to be able to download the presentation during the webcast. It is available for download in the **Event Details** section during the webcast

### Webcast Presentations and Best Practices

Some PowerPoint best practices for display during a webcast are suggested below.

**Rev Related:\&#xA;**

* Embed fonts
* Remove all animations
* Consider previewing your slides at the lowest resolution you are broadcasting at

**General Best Practices:**

* Simplify slides and limit the amount of text on each screen
* Use large fonts for legibility
* Make sure that there is enough contrast between text and backgrounds
* If you want to additional information bullets on a slide, use separate slides to do so
* It is best to preview your slides on the screen that you will be presenting on from the back of the room
