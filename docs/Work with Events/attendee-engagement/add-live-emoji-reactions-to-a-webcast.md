You can let your attendees express how they feel about content in your Webcast by enabling **Live Emoji Reactions** in the **Attendee Engagements** section.

<Image alt="Five emojis are pre-selected by default when you enable Live Emoji Reactions for an event" align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/09760074cb136c5efb2b1d798c6e88a449b5f4d38e3d6206a63385ceff98cec8-enableLiveEmojiReactions.png">
  Five emojis are pre-selected by default when you enable Live Emoji Reactions for an event
</Image>

You are able to enable five reactions (emojis) at a time for an event and there are five (pictured above) that are selected for you by default.  To remove an emoji, click the X to the right of its name and it will clear it from use.

> 👍 Tip
>
> This setting is also saved in Webcast templates and when you duplicate an event.

To add a new emoji, click the **Plus (+)** icon and select the emoji you want to use from the drop-down that appears.  You must have space to add the emoji, meaning, only five may be used at any one time for an event. You may change these (or disable them entirely) [during an ongoing webcast](doc:edit-settings-during-an-ongoing-webcast) in the **Attendee Engagements** section.

<Image align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/46002e155c5b8d21d44edcf00fed061651beaf99128e0bfb0538cfe1286e800c-addNewEmoji.png" />

When emojis are enabled for a Webcast, attendees can use the **Reactions** icon in player controls to express how they feel about the content you are displaying within the interval you have defined during event setup. 

<Image alt="While the Webcast is in progress, attendees can use the Reactions icon and use the emojis you have enabled to &#x22;react&#x22; to what they are viewing" align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/1d6413bbc35df981240a0bded98f98fb6c678bb6c0a30fff5cdc04ca6f45e920-reactionsControl.png">
  While the Webcast is in progress, attendees can use the Reactions icon and use the emojis you have enabled to "react" to what they are viewing
</Image>

It is important to note that the reactions/emojis that attendees use are *not* attributed to them. Further, attendees can hide Reactions if they are found to be intrusive while viewing a Webcast.

<Image alt="Reactions are not attributed to any one individual account and may be hidden at any time" align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/fabce0cec34bee04daf9cbf030ea7ef1a1a46ba30de189f63a19d403dcff1b0f-webcastReactions.png">
  Reactions are not attributed to any one individual account and may be hidden at any time
</Image>

You can [download a reaction/emoji report](doc:download-a-webcast-reactions-emoji-report) at the conclusion of your event to understand how your attendees used them and where.

> 📘 Note
>
> This feature must be [globally enabled in Media Settings](doc:allow-webcast-features) before it may be added to your events.
