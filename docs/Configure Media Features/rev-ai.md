Vbrick's **Rev IQ** solution uses generative Artificial Intelligence (AI) and machine learning to help employees find specific video content they need across all of your video assets.  This means viewers can search and find videos about _who_ appears and _what_ is being said using **Smart Search** and **Facial Recognition**. 

Rev also uses AI-powered **Smart Search** by default to improve the relevance and quality of search results. AI-powered search improves users’ ability to discover video assets by enabling algorithms that identify _concepts_ rather than relying solely on keywords. This results in more accurate search results.

Specifically, **AI & Machine Learning** features help viewers find the relevant content they need in the portal and turn _content into actionable information_.  Here are some examples of what you can do using Rev's AI and Machine Language features:

- [Facial Recognition](doc:facial-recognition) - Rev IQ automatically tags users in a video so that viewers can find the videos they are in and jump to the moment they appear.  Quickly find the speakers and information you need.
- [Content Analysis Workflows](doc:content-analysis-workflows) - This feature, when enabled, allows you to add an AI-content analysis step to a **Content Approval Process** you have defined for your video uploads.
- [Interactive Transcript Viewing](doc:rev-video-player-features#video-transcript) - Allows you to follow along to see who is in the video and what is being said as it is playing in real-time.
- [Rev IQ Transcription and Translation](doc:rev-iq-transcription-and-translation) - Create transcripts for videos that then power closed captions and voice generation the ability to search for specific content in them. Includes the ability to create custom dictionaries for your portal. Transcripts are needed for both AI features and can then be translated into several [Supported Languages](doc:supported-languages).

You must purchase [Rev AI Hours](doc:rev-license-types-and-add-ons#licensed-add-on-components) licensing components to use this feature's functions. Speak to your Account Manager for details.
