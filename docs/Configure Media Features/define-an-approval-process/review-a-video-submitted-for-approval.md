You have the ability to approve or reject video submissions as an **Approver** in a content Approval Process. Before the submission can be made Public or proceed to the next step in the process (if one exists), at least *one* Approver in the step must approve it. 

To review a video submitted for approval:

1. [Find Videos That Need Approval](doc:find-videos-that-need-approval) and choose one you want to review. The current step is noted as well as how many steps there are (in total) to the Approval Process the video is part of.  The image below depicts the video is on the final step (step two) of a two step approval process for example.

2. Review the **Video Settings** as needed to make sure it complies with your organization's standards. You can also use the **Review** icon to access the **Review flyout** panel. This panel allows you to leave specific comments to the submitter and other approvers that may follow you, and include a timestamp where the comment is relevant to provide more context.

<Image align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/d835652-commentTimestamp.png" />

3. If you have [AI-Content Analysis](doc:ai-content-analysis) enabled, you can review any violations or flags on the **Compliance Alert** tab.  Notice that the tab specifies exactly where in the video the violation occurs with a corresponding icon on the timeline itself.

<Image alt="AI Content Analysis flags sensitive data and keywords you have set (if enabled) and shows you where on the timeline they occur" align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/a0e4b53-complianceAlertTab.png">
  AI Content Analysis flags sensitive data and keywords you have set (if enabled) and shows you where on the timeline they occur
</Image>

> 👍 Tip
>
> An AI generated alert versus a manually entered comment on the timeline will have different colored visual indicators and icons.

4. Click the **Approve** or **Reject** button once you have concluded your review. Enter an approval (or rejection) message, if desired. 

<Image alt="Including a message why a video is rejected is helpful" align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/e7fba31-rejectNote.png">
  Including a message why a video is rejected is helpful
</Image>

5. This message is emailed to the owner/submitter of the video so that it may be corrected and resubmitted if rejected. It is also included in the **Notification** the submitter receives.

<Image align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/ba332bb-submitterNoticeofRejection.png" />

6. If a video is **rejected**, it *must* be **resubmitted** and approved before it may move to the next Approval Step (if there is more than one step).
7. If the video is approved, it is set to **Active** status and is viewable by other users of the portal.

> 📘 Note
>
> Account and Media Admins are able to view all **Rejected** videos when viewing **Inactive** status videos.
>
> Account and Media admins are also able to approve/reject on behalf of an Approver if needed. This prevents an Approver from going on vacation and leaving a video in **Pending Approval** status.
