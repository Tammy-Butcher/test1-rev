The next stage of your content Approval Process should be to define the **Approval Steps**.  This includes specifying the Users and Groups who approve the content submitted for approval and **AI-Content analysis** steps to include, if enabled.

> 👍 Tip
> 
> You can create up to five separate approval steps if needed. The number next to the step indicates the order it is executed.  To reorder your steps, click the up and down arrows and Rev reorders your step(s) automatically for you.

## Who is Approving the Content?

Begin by entering an **Approvers Step Name**. Every step is _required_ to have a name.

Enter the Users and Groups that will act as content Approvers in the **Select Users/Groups as Approvers** box.

> 👍 Tip
> 
> Approvers are **required** to approve or reject the submitted video before it may be viewed by other users on the Portal. 
> 
> However, if more than one approver is designated, only _one_ approver needs to approve the content before it "passes" the stage.

> 📘 Note
> 
> If the **Suppress Email** control is checked next to the Approver, that account(s) will not receive pending _approval_ emails. 
> 
> _Rejection_ emails are still sent. This function may be set at all approval stages.

Click the **Add Approvers** button at the bottom of the form to add additional approval steps. You may create up to 5 approval steps in an approval process. A video upload is required to pass **all **steps before it is visible on the portal.
