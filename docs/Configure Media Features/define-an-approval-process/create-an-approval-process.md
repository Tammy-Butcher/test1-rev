The content **Approval Process** you create can be a single-layered, simple approach or you can add multiple workflow steps. To get started:

1. Navigate to **Media Settings > Approval Processes**. You must be an Account Admin or Media Admin for this access.
2. Click the **Create Approval Process** button.  If you are editing a previously created process, click its name to access its steps and attributes.

## Who Requires Content Approval?

Specify _who_ requires approval when they upload content in the **Groups/Users Requiring Approval** box.  This means the **Groups** or **Users** in this box will require that their content go through the process defined here before their content is visible.

You can also toggle the **Include All Users** switch which means that _anyone_ who uploads content requires approval through this process.  If you specify to include everyone, then you do not need to include individual accounts or groups since everyone who uploads content will need to be approved.
