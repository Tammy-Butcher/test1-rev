A **Content Approval Process** specifies that certain groups and users must have videos approved before they are viewed by other users on the portal. You can also dictate that certain data is analyzed before approval is granted using Rev's AI features. 

Rev's content approval process allows you to create a simple, single-layered approach, or a multi-step process with different stages and approvers depending upon the needs of your organization. You must have **Admin Account** or **Media Admin** access to [create or edit an approval process](doc:create-an-approval-process) and **Rev IQ credits** if you use content-centric approval steps as described below.

## Use Case Examples

The following use cases demonstrate various methods and approval workflows you might consider.

### Subject Matter Expert Approval (Person Approval)

You have a Subject Matter Expert in your organization that is a trainer.  All videos that are uploaded, and categorized under this training tag, must be approved by this person.  This means that a content approval process should be created with this person as the main Approver before they are made public on the portal for viewing.

### New Hire Approval (Group / Person Approval)

New employees responsible for marketing videos are placed in a New Hire group with experienced users set as Approvers in a New Hire approval process. All uploads created by this group are subject to approval by the experienced users and video status is set to Pending Approval. When the employee is deemed experienced enough, he/she is removed from this group and no longer subject to approval for uploading.

Additionally, the approval process may be a multi-step approach if different organizations need to be involved. For example, you may decide that a video needs to go through both the Sales team and Human Resources as well before it is approved for public consumption (made Active).

### Content Data Approval (AI Content Analysis Approval)

If you have Rev IQ credits and [AI content moderation](doc:generative-ai-tools) enabled, you can add a content moderation step to either of the above workflows to check for sensitive data and/or specified keywords in your videos. Rev's AI-powered content analysis streamlines your content moderation process by detecting sensitive data (such as financials) and keywords you define (such as hate speech) automatically.  This reduces human error and takes the guesswork out of compliance within your approval process when it comes to safeguarding your content and brand.
