If content subscriptions are enabled for your portal, the **Subscribe** button is visible on each individual category page when you access the **Media** > **Browse Categories** menu.

<Image align="center" alt={1835} border={false} caption="Click on a category to access its subscription options" title="subscribeCategory.png" src="https://files.readme.io/9a7a708-subscribeCategory.png" />

When you click the **Subscribe** button, you are able to toggle a **System Notification** for the category which means that you will receive an email each time a new video is added to the category as well as a portal [notification](doc:notifications) flag.

<Image align="center" alt={290} border={false} caption="Clicking the subscribe button presents subscription options that have been enabled, including notifications and Webex Teams" title="unsubscribe.png" src="https://files.readme.io/7935881-unsubscribe.png" />

If [Webex Teams](doc:webex-teams) integration has been set up, you can also subscribe the category to your Webex Teams space so that members of the space are notified when new content is added.

> 📘 Note
>
> When subscribing a category to a **Webex Teams** space, you will need to be logged in to that space.  View [Subscribe a Rev Category to a Webex Teams Space](doc:webex-teams#subscribe-a-rev-category-to-a-webex-team-space) for complete details on how to do this.

Once the category subscription has been added it can be revisited and the **Unsubscribe** button can be clicked to either unsubscribe entirely or turn off system notifications.  To subscribe to a subcategory, you must visit that individual subcategory page and follow the same procedure.

> 🚧 Important!
>
> If you toggle off email and/or Webex Teams notifications on the [Manage Subscriptions](doc:manage-my-subscriptions) page, you are effectively unsubscribing from the **Category** in addition to unsubscribing from emails.  You will need to return here and subscribe again to receive the category feed on the [My Subscriptions](doc:my-subscriptions) page.

View the **Media** > [My Subscriptions](doc:my-subscriptions) page to view a feed of all content related to your subscribed categories.
