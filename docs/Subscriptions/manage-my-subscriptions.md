The **Manage Subscriptions** link under your [user profile](doc:your-rev-profile) drop-down provides you a list of the [channels](doc:subscribe-to-a-channel) and [categories](doc:subscribe-to-a-category) you are subscribed to sorted by alphabetical order.

Unsubscribe from a channel or category by toggling its email notification off.  Keep in mind that if you unsubscribe, you will need to subscribe again by returning to the specific [channel](doc:subscribe-to-a-channel) or [category](doc:subscribe-to-a-category) to do so.

If [Webex Teams](doc:webex-teams) subscriptions are enabled, you will need to log in to the team you have been assigned before you are able to modify subscription options.

> 👍 Tip
> 
> You can also go directly to the category or channel and click the **Unsubscribe** button from there.
