The **My Subscriptions** page under the [Media](doc:user-menu-options) menu provides a content feed from the [categories](doc:subscribe-to-a-category) and [channels](doc:subscribe-to-a-channel) you are subscribed to.

When you view **My Subscriptions**:

* The content is filtered based on the channels and categories that you are subscribed to
* The videos must be **Active** and will be sorted by upload date descending (most recent)
* The channel videos displayed are only those from the channels you belong to
* You can view content by **tile** or **list** view
* [Bulk Editing](doc:bulk-edit-video-settings) options are available
* Clicking into a video and clicking back will retain the search filters 
* If a channel or category is deleted or if a video is removed from a channel or category, it is no longer displayed in the subscriptions feed
* Use the [Manage Subscriptions](doc:manage-my-subscriptions) button to manage each subscription option and unsubscribe if desired
