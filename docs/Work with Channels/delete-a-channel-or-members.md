Rev Admins \_and \_Channel Admins have the Delete icon present on the Channels module next to the Channel Name.

_Only_ **Channel Admins** may delete members in the Channel itself by clicking the X control.  This illustrates why it is important to designate this role immediately.  Rev Admin roles do not have permissions within the Channel.
