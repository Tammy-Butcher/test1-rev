Rev Admins \_and \_Channel Admins have the Delete icon present on the Channels module next to the Channel Name.

<Image title="deleteChannel.png" alt={747} align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/232dbf9-deleteChannel.png">
  Both Rev Account Admin and Media Admin roles may delete a Channel along with the Channel Admin
</Image>

*Only* **Channel Admins** may delete members in the Channel itself by clicking the X control.  This illustrates why it is important to designate this role immediately.  Rev Admin roles do not have permissions within the Channel.

<Image title="deleteChannelMember.png" alt={257} align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/38254a4-deleteChannelMember.png">
  Only the Channel Admin may delete Channel Members
</Image>
