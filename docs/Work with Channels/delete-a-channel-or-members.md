Rev Admins and Channel Admins have the **Delete** icon present on the Channels module next to the **Channel Name** in the Admin menu.

<Image title="deleteChannel.png" alt="Both Rev Account Admin and Media Admin roles may delete a Channel along with the Channel Admin" align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/276144b2f447f6bbbfcb400b0ed5abbb50faa0964574dee97e64bd95e8f38691-deleteChannelAdminMenu.png">
  Both Rev Account Admin and Media Admin roles may delete a Channel along with the Channel Admin
</Image>

The channel may also be deleted on the Channel Landing Page by clicking the **Delete** button.

<Image align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/5e906e6a3bf73b12d2f49c6d92cef5d53b73f54e5002ff7ea6454db294e795b9-deleteChannelLandingPage.png" />

*Only* **Channel Admins** may delete members in the Channel itself by clicking the X control next to the member.  This illustrates why it is *important* to designate a Channel Admin role immediately.  Rev Admin roles *do not* have permissions within the Channel to manage their members.

<Image title="deleteChannelMember.png" alt="Only the Channel Admin may delete Channel Members" align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/134fa5083aa37c985da3bcf73176a423d9e4d8e3551fbf6215d42e7095e5346f-deleteChannelMember.png">
  Only the Channel Admin may delete Channel Members
</Image>
