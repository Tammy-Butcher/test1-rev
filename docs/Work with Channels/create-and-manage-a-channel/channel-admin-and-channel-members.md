Use the **Assign Users and Groups **control to assign a **Channel Admin** and add **Users **and **Groups **to your channel "membership".  Your members should also be assigned appropriate roles.

To add Channel members:

1. Type the **User **or **Group ** you want to assign to the channel in the **Find Items** control and click **Done **to add them.

2. The **Channel Member** role is assigned initially to every added member. This means they can view all channel content and attend events that the channel is assigned to. You can change this role by clicking on it and selecting a different role from one of the drop-down options that appears. But first, assign the channel's **Channel Admin**.

3. Assign the person (or group) responsible for managing the channel by assigning the **Channel Admin** immediately.  _Only_ the Channel Admin may [modify the channel roles and settings](doc:channel-roles-and-permissions) so this is important to update right away.

Channel Admins have the same rights as an Account Admin but only for videos assigned to that specific Channel. This includes:

- Ability to edit channel settings
- Ability to bulk edit videos in the channel
- Ability to approve or reject videos in the channel that are part of an approval process

4. Once your Channel Admin is assigned, you should decide which channel members are **Channel Uploaders** versus **Channel Contributors**. 
   1. The main difference is that the uploader has _all the permissions_ of the Channel Contributor with the exception of this role has **no** access to the **Live Recording** tab on the channel's **Upload** tab. For complete details on channel roles and permissions view the [Channel Granular Roles and Permissions](doc:channel-roles-and-permissions#channel-granular-roles-and-permissions) topic.
