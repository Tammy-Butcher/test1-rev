Use the **Assign Users and Groups **control to assign a **Channel Admin** and add **Users **and **Groups **to your channel "membership".  Your members are also assigned roles.

To add Channel members:

1. Type the **User **or **Group **in the **Find Items** control and click **Done **to add them.

2. The **Channel Member** role is assigned initially to every added member which means they can view all channel content and attend events that the channel is assigned to. 

3. Assign the person (or group) responsible for managing the Channel by assigning the **Channel Admin** immediately.  Only the Channel Admin may [modify the channel roles and settings](doc:roles-and-permissions#section-channel-role-definitions-and-permissions) so this is important.

Channel Admins have the same rights as an Account Admin but only for videos assigned to that specific Channel. This includes:

* Ability to edit channel settings
* Ability to bulk edit videos in the channel
* Ability to approve or reject videos in the channel that are part of an approval process
