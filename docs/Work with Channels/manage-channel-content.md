**Channels** in Rev are managed by the various members depending upon the [role they are assigned](doc:roles-and-permissions#section-channel-role-definitions-and-permissions) when it is created or when they are added.  A **Channel Member**, for example, can only view content that is uploaded while a **Channel Contributor** can add new content. 

## View All Channels

When the **Media** > **View All** Channels menu option is chosen, all channels that you are a member of appear. Be aware that this option is only available and appears if you are a member of one or more channels in Rev.

<Image title="allChannelsLanding.png" alt="The All Channels page displays only the channels that you are a member of" align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/0369b36402ce500edd3d0238371a8e154dd0d76cd388d9da683cc3357b2f1c30-viewAllChannels.png">
  The All Channels page displays only the channels that you are a member of
</Image>

The **Channel** landing page allows you to easily search channels you are a member of and create a new channel if you have the permissions to do so.

Each channel tile displays:

* Channel Name
* Number of videos in the channel

Click a tile to navigate to the **Channel Landing Page**.

## The Channel Landing Page

Clicking a Channel from **Media** > **Channels**  page or dropdown displays an individual channel's **Channel Landing Page**.

The Channel Landing Page displays:

* Channel Name
* Description (if entered)
* The last time the channel was updated with either a video or member (found under the **More** hyperlink)
* The number of videos assigned to the channel (also found under the **More** hyperlink)
* If you are a **Channel Admin** (or have permissions to edit or contribute to channels), the following buttons are available to manage the channel:
  * An **Edit Channel** button that works similarly to creating the channel in that it modifies channel metadata such as Name, Description, etc. and also allows you to add Users and Groups to the channel.
  * A **Delete Channel** button that will remove the channel.  This cannot be undone so use with caution.
  * An **Upload** button that is similar to adding video to Rev. In this case, however, it is adding video only to the channel itself so that only channel members view it.
* Filter, sort, and bulk editing options that function for channel videos.
* A [Subscribe](doc:subscribe-to-a-channel) button (if enabled) or an Unsubscribe button if already subscribed.

<Image title="channelsLanding.png" alt="The Channel Landing page displays all videos uploaded to that channel along with sort, filter, and bulk editing options" align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/41f6a1fc74601c6445dfbed5a50b0380a601beea4c0b43c99b89d868c39124ba-channelLandingPage.png">

> 👍 Tip
>
> Hover over a video in Grid view to preview it.  Right-clicking on a video allows you to open it in a new tab or window.

## Upload or Record Video for a Channel

<Image title="uploadToChannels.png" align="center" width="smart" src="https://files.readme.io/1faab14e9beda01d12310fd9cfd8b56208c9fb4e9711c4b591472ae05af5e01e-channelUploadButton.png" />

**Channel Contributors** or **Channel Admins** may upload or record videos directly from the **Channel Landing Page** using the **Upload to Channel** icon. 

When a video is added from the Channels page, the [Access Control](doc:video-access-control) is automatically set to **Channels** and assigned to the channel you are uploading or recording from.  This means that only Channel Members may view the video.

*Only* Channel Admins and Channel Contributors may add a video to a Channel Landing page.

After clicking the **Upload** icon, [add or record video](doc:adding-video) as you normally would.

## Channel Landing Page Video Filters

Use Rev’s video [search filters](doc:search-and-filter-functions) to search for videos containing specific attributes if your **Channel Landing Page** contains several videos. Use the **Filters** icon to access this functionality.

## Bulk Edit Channel Videos

<Image align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/b9055dd21bdae1f7fa0d996643030ed3305c68b56b60d6297d1d1653fe027ee0-bulkEdit.png" />

Use Rev’s **bulk edit** feature to modify several video settings at once that appear on your Channel Landing Page if you have edit permissions.

To use the [Bulk Edit](doc:bulk-edit-video-settings) feature on the Channel Landing Page:

1. Click the **Bulk Edit** icon to open the bulk editing interface. 

<Image title="bulkEditChannelVideos.png" alt="Select all the videos to include in your bulk edit and click the Edit Videos icon" align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/f9f993404f76d83a618cff17e2118ac4ad8c72a1d0bd92eadb54fecc6f533b54-bulkEditChannelVids.png">
  Select all the videos to include in your bulk edit and click the Edit Videos icon
</Image>

2. Select the videos you want to edit and click the **Edit Settings** icon.

3. The [Video Settings](doc:update-basic-video-settings) you may edit appear. Make your desired edits and click the **Save** button.
