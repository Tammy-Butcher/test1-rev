**Channels** in Rev are managed by the various members depending upon the [role they are assigned](doc:roles-and-permissions#section-channel-role-definitions-and-permissions) when it is created or when they are added.  A **Channel Member**, for example, can only view content that is uploaded while a **Channel Contributor** can add new content. 

## View All Channels

When the **Media** > **My Channels** > **View All Channels** menu option is chosen, all channels that you are a member of appear. This option is only available if you are a member of one or more channels in Rev.

<Image title="allChannelsLanding.png" alt={1812} align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/c21ae7b-allChannelsLanding.png">
  The All Channels page displays only the channels that you are a member of
</Image>

The **Channels** landing page allows you to easily search channels you are a member of and create a new channel if you have the permissions to do so.

Each channel tile displays:

* Channel Name
* Number of videos in the channel

Click a tile to navigate to the **Channel Landing Page**.

## The Channel Landing Page

Clicking a Channel from **Media** > **My Channels** displays an individual **Channel Landing Page**.

The Channel Landing Page displays:

* Channel Name
* The last time the Channel was updated with either a video or member
* An **Edit Channel** link (if you are an Admin account or have been assigned permissions to edit the Channel)
* All videos assigned to the Channel
* Filter, sort, and bulk editing options
* A [Subscribe](doc:subscribe-to-a-channel) button (if enabled)

<Image title="channelsLanding.png" alt={1860} align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/060de58-channelsLanding.png">
  The Channel Landing page displays all videos uploaded to that channel along with sort, filter, and bulk editing options
</Image>

> 👍 Tip
>
> Hover over a video in Grid view to preview it.  Right-clicking on a video allows you to open it in a new tab or window.

## Upload or Record Video for a Channel

<Image title="uploadToChannels.png" alt={104} align="center" width="smart" src="https://files.readme.io/f47d0a3-uploadToChannels.png" />

**Channel Contributors** or **Channel Admins** may upload or record videos directly from the **Channel Landing Page** using the **Upload to Channel** icon. 

When a video is added from the Channels page, the [Access Control](doc:update-basic-video-settings#video-access-control) is automatically set to **Channels** and assigned to the channel you are uploading or recording from.  This means that only Channel Members may view the video.

*Only* Channel Admins and Channel Contributors may add a video to a Channel Landing page.

After clicking the **Upload** icon, [add or record video](doc:adding-video) as you normally would.

## Channel Landing Page Video Filters

Use Rev’s video [search filters](doc:search-and-filter-functions) to search for videos containing specific attributes if your **Channel Landing Page** contains several videos. Use the **Filters** icon to access this functionality.

<Image title="channelFilters.png" alt={335} align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/e97d174-channelFilters.png">
  Use Rev's filter functions to find specific videos in your Channel
</Image>

## Bulk Edit Channel Videos

<Image align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/46fb745-bulkEdit.png" />

Use Rev’s **bulk edit** feature to modify several video settings at once that appear on your Channel Landing Page if you have edit permissions.

To use the [Bulk Edit](doc:bulk-edit-video-settings) feature on the Channel Landing Page:

1. Click the **Bulk Edit** icon to open the bulk editing interface. 

<Image title="bulkEditChannelVideos.png" alt={1855} align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/59848b9-bulkEditChannelVideos.png">
  Select all the videos to include in your bulk edit and click the Edit Videos icon
</Image>

2. Select the videos you want to edit and click the **Edit Settings** icon.

3. The [Video Settings](doc:update-basic-video-settings) you may edit appear. Make your desired edits and click the **Save** button.
