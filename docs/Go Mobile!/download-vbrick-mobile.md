The **Vbrick Mobile** app is designed for on-demand playback and provides the ability to search for videos, browse channels and categories, and upload videos from Android or iOS devices. All custom settings, including branding, logo, and accent colors, are displayed in the app.

> 👍 Tip
> 
> You must have an account and a valid email address to your company's Rev cloud portal before you can use the Vbrick Mobile app.

Specifically, with the app, you can:

- Watch on-demand videos
- View a carousel of featured videos
- Browse feeds of recommended and most recent content
- Search for videos
- Scan categories and channels to find videos of interest
- Join Live webcasts using a webcast browser
- Upload videos captured on mobile device (Media Contributors role only) 

**Vbrick Mobile** is available for download on Google Play and in the Apple Store.
