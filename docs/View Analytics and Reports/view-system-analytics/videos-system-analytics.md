The **Videos **tab on the Account Admin Dashboard details viewing and completion rate trends for all of the videos in the portal based on the Date Range you select.

Four Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) provide a quick view of video statistics:
* Views
* Unique Viewers
* Average Viewing Time (duration)
* Completion rate when viewing videos

## Video Views

The **Views **chart displays the total views based on the **Date Range** you select in the date control. For date ranges less than 90 days, the x-axis interval is by day. For date ranges greater than 90 days, the x-axis interval is 7 days. Rolling the mouse over a specific day in the chart displays that day’s views

## Export Video Analytics Reports

The **Top 50 Videos** and **Views **tabs are also filtered by the **Date Range** you select. A sorting and filtering **Columns **control for each allows you to filter additional key views metrics for each that you specifically want to observe without the need to download the Video report(s) first.

Note you may still download complete reports for both should you so choose by clicking the **CSV **button.
