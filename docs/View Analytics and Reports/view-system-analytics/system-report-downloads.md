The **Admin Account Dashboard** reports can be exported into CSV Excel spreadsheets to save and review. Further, the spreadsheets have more details that may be desirable to you than simply viewing them on the dashboard.

## Download the Top 50 Videos and Views Report(s)

To download the complete **Top 50 Video** and/or **Views** Report(s), click the **CSV** tab next to the **Date** control on the **Videos** tab.

Use the **Columns** control to simply view the data as opposed to downloading the data.

>
> If the **Hide User-Level Analytics** checkbox is enabled under [Content Restrictions](doc:hide-user-level-analytics), neither the **Views** tab nor the **Views CSV** file export in the image above is displayed to any account, including Admin accounts.
