When a Vbrick Rev user account is set up for you by your Account Admin, you will receive an email confirmation at the email address that is used. You can click on the link in the email to create your Rev user account at that time.

![](https://files.readme.io/b187ea0-newUserConfirmation.png "newUserConfirmation.png")

Before the **Submit** button may be clicked, a **Password** and **Security Question** must be created for your account. All fields are required before your account registration is complete and you may login to Rev.

## Reset Your Password

If you forget your user account password, click the **(Forgot Password?)** hyperlink on the sign in page. You will be prompted for your Username.

An email will be sent to your email address on file that contains a hyperlink where you may reset your user password using the **Forgot Password** form. You will be unable to log in until you complete this form. If you do not receive the email, contact your Account Admin.

Keep in mind that you will need to know the answer to your **Security Question** you created to reset your password.

<Image title="forgotPassword.png" alt={781} align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/cd0fe3f-forgotPassword.png">
  You must know your Security Question to reset your password
</Image>

You may also be prompted to reset your password if your Account Admin has a password expiration policy in place. For example, your organization may require that your reset your password every 90 days. If this is the case, when your 90 days elapse, you will receive an **Expired Password** log-in notification at the log-in screen.  In this case, you are required to use the **Security Question** you set during registration to reset your password before you can access your account again.

<Image alt="If your password expires you are required to use your Security Question to reset your password" align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/7e59ecfdd0cfd0564ed1940a23ca5bd8bbddc922548fc7b14873182875af9c6f-expiredPasswordLogin.png">
  If your password expires you are required to use your Security Question to reset your password
</Image>

You may also [change your password](doc:your-rev-profile#change-your-account-password) at any time if your Account Admin has password expirations enabled through your [Rev Profile settings](doc:your-rev-profile).

<Image align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/35b8a780e4f36acc37914d99aaf4c0b0c878b5b1538b0280eb261f334b4cf6da-changeProfilePassword.png" />

## Reset Your Security Question

If you enter the wrong answer for your **Security Question** and reach the limit on password reset attempts, you will locked out of your account as a security measure.  At that time, you will need to contact an Account Admin to reset your account.

<Image title="passwordResetLimit.png" alt={863} align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/4dee370-passwordResetLimit.png">
  Password limit reached
</Image>

You will be sent another verification email that will let you use the **New User Confirmation** form to create a new **Password** and **Security Question** similar to when you first created your account once your Account Admin resets your password for you.
