When a Vbrick Rev user account is created by your Account Admin, an email confirmation is sent to the email address that is used.

![](https://files.readme.io/b187ea0-newUserConfirmation.png "newUserConfirmation.png")



Before the **Submit** button may be clicked, a **Password **and **Security Question** must be completed for your account. All fields are required before your account registration is complete and you may login to Rev.

## Reset Your Password

If you have forgotten your user account password, click the **(Forgot Password?)** hyperlink on the Sign In form. You will be prompted for your Username.

An email will be sent to your email address on file that contains a hyperlink where you may reset your user password using the **Forgot Password** form. You will be unable to log in until you complete this form. If you do not receive the email, contact your Account Admin.

Keep in mind that you will need to know the answer to your **Security Question** to reset your password.

## Reset Your Security Question

If you enter the wrong answer for your **Security Question** and reach the limit on password reset attempts, you will need to contact an Account Admin to reset your account.

You will be sent another verification email that will let you use the **New User Confirmation** form to create a new **Password **and **Security Question** similar to when you first created your account once your Account Admin resets your password for you.
