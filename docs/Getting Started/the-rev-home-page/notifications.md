The **Notifications **icon immediately alerts you upon log in (and in real-time while logged in) that certain actions occurred in Rev. 

For example, when you first log in, the **Notifications** icon informs you (by number and background color) that you have new or unread actions since your last log in if this is the case. The color informs you of the type of action that has occurred. 

A blue icon (seen below) is an alert that indicates nine successful actions have occurred and your videos are most likely ready to edit. If the notification icon is red, this indicates that your immediate attention is needed on an action that has occurred.

Clicking the **Mark all as read** link clears all notifications and resets the icon.

Clicking the **See all notifications** link displays the Notifications form with more details about each. You may click it at any time to also view past notifications. The Notifications form is capped at 100 notifications.

Keep in mind that you can click on a specific notification to be taken to the event that occurred.  For example, Rev alerts you if someone replies to a comment you leave on a video, (seen in the image below). Clicking this notification takes you directly to the video where the comment was left.

Notifications appear in chronological order with the newest notification listed first and sorted by date and time. Clicking of a specific notification takes you directly to that video.

> 👍 Tip
> 
> Remember, click the **See all notifications** hyperlink at any time to view notifications that have taken place in the past even if you have no current notifications to view. 
> 
> Some notifications send you an email if you have an email address on file.
