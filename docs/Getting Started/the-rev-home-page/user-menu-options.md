View videos based on arranged tiles or in a list based on **Upload Date** once a selection is made by selecting the **List **or **Tile** buttons. You can hover over a video while in Tile view to preview it. Right-click on a video in either view to open it in a new tab or window.

The **Sort **icon (tile-view only) will sort by **Recommended**, **Title**, **Upload Date**, and **Views**. Recommendations are based on popularity, content freshness and the user’s viewing history.

The **Filter **icon may be used to search further on specific video attributes.

## My Channels

The **Channels** option under the **Media **menu contains each channel you are a member of and the ability to **View All** channels you are a member of in the channel box. 

Click a channel to go to that specific **Channel Landing** page only. If you are a Rev Account or Media Admin (or are a Channel Creator) you have the [Add New Channel](doc:create-and-manage-a-channel) option.

View the [Channels](doc:manage-channel-content) guide for details on managing channels in Rev.

## The Event Menu

Clicking the **Event **menu opens the [Event Calendar](doc:the-event-calendar) which displays all upcoming Webcasts that you also have access to view and allows you to create your own with the correct permissions. 

By default, the calendar is displayed in **Monthly **view but you may toggle between a monthly, weekly, and daily based view. Click the **Today **button to quickly navigate back to the current day.
