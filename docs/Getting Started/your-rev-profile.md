You can update your profile settings and profile image at any time by clicking the user account icon in the [Header Navigation](doc:the-rev-home-page#header-navigation-options) options.

<Image title="userProfileIcon.png" alt="User account profile icon and dropdown" align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/80616e6d1a8bc00df7f20c9ddc326a3f86ceeb3ee01fe8022c9e4236141dadac-profileDropdown.png">
  User account profile icon and dropdown
</Image>

This icon also allows you to [Manage Subscriptions](doc:manage-my-subscriptions) you may have and to logout of Rev.

## Update Your Profile Settings

When you click the user account icon, the **My Profile** option appears from the dropdown menu. You are able to update the following information:

<Image title="user_profile.png" alt={728} align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/da2e118-user_profile.png">
  Update your Rev user profile settings
</Image>

* First Name
* Last Name
* Contact Email (should be a non-duplicate valid email address if used)
* Title
* Phone Number
* Preferred Language

## Change Your Account Password

If your Account Admin has enabled Rev's password expiration feature, you have the ability to change your Rev password at any time from your profile settings. Just above your profile image, in the **Password** section, the amount of time left you have before your password expires is displayed. You may click the provided link to change your password at any time.

<Image alt="The Password section appears on your Rev Profile if your Admin has enabled password expirations" align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/8547215feb601e7940038e5a2292e82414bb81151c5795cc04ee2e5940c1b83b-changeProfilePassword.png">
  The Password section appears on your Rev Profile if your Admin has enabled password expirations
</Image>

## Update Your Profile Image

<Image title="updateProfile.png" alt={886} align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/6415b40-updateProfile.png">
  Rev user profile image
</Image>

If you upload a profile image, it will be used in the following areas of the Rev portal:

* Header Navigation Menus (Profile icons)
* Webcast Events
  * Attendee List
  * Chat
  * Q\&A
* Videos
  * Video Owner (if you upload a video or are assigned as an owner)
  * Comments (if you are the Commenter)
* Administration & Search
  * User Management
  * Whenever searching for a user
* Facial Recognition & Tagging

> 📘 Note
>
> If you do **not** want to be included in Rev’s [In This Video](doc:rev-video-player-features#video-pulse) and **Facial Recognition** searches and tagging, you may opt-out by selecting the **Do not tag my face in videos uploaded to the Rev platform** checkbox.

> 👍 Tip
>
> A recommended resolution for profile pictures is 480x480 and no larger than 5MB. A valid file format for use with Rev must also be used.
