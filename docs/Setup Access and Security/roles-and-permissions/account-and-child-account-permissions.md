Creating and managing Rev [Child Accounts](doc:view-and-edit-account-details#portal-child-accounts) (also known as sub-accounts) is strictly limited to the Account Admin role in Rev as well as editing your main Rev account. No other role has these permissions at present.  Included in account and sub-account permissions is the ability to:

* Edit (Rev) account details
* Add a (Child) sub-account
* Edit a (Child) sub-account
* Delete a (Child) sub-account
