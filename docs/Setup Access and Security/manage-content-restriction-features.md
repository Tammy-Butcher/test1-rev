**System Settings** in Rev help determine how security is handled for both users and your portal content with functions such as password settings, SSO, and private video access. This is also where API Keys are created and managed for your device configurations.

**Content Restriction** allows you to place controls on specific pieces of content or even entire areas and functions in Rev depending on your business model.

As with all menu options in Rev, your level of access and visibility depends on your account and permission settings. **System Settings** are accessed through the [Admin Menu Options](doc:admin-menu-options).
