Rev supports content approval processes that may be put in place by your Account Admins to ensure that content is reviewed and is appropriate before it is released. If you upload a video and its [status](doc:video-status) is set to **Approval Required**, you need to submit your video through a content approval process. 

Videos that need approval are _only_  seen by the **Video Owner **and Rev **Admins **until they are approved. The approval process may have one or more stages depending on the process put in place by your Account Admins.

To submit a video for approval:

1. Upload or add a video as you normally would and then edit the **Video Settings** as needed for publication.

2. When ready, click the **Submit For Approval **button. If more the one approval process exists for a user, group, or channel, a drop-down menu is present that allows you to select the appropriate process for the content you are uploading.

> 📘 Note
> 
> If your organization does not use Rev's AI features, you can skip to step 8 at this time.

3. If [AI-Content Analysis](doc:ai-content-analysis) has been enabled for your Approval Process, you will see a "Video content analysis still processing" message prompt if you view the video before the analysis is complete.
4. Once the analysis has concluded, use the **Review** icon to open the **Review flyout panel**.  This flyout panel allows you to review your video for any sensitive data or keywords your Admin has flagged that should not be included. 
   1. **Sensitive data** includes personally identifiable information such as financial information, social security card, address and so forth.
   2. **Keywords** are words that your Admin has set up that should not be released Publicly. They may change over time.
5. Flagged icons on the **Compliance Alerts** tab are also correlated with a matching icon on your video timeline so you know exactly where in the video you need to review.
6. Similar to the Compliance tab, you can also use the **Comments** tab to note in the video specific incidents that you want to point out to any Approvers. Note you have the option to include timestamps if you'd like to associate your manual comment with a specific moment in time in the video.

7. When ready, click the **Approve **button to proceed through the AI-Content analysis step and proceed to the next submission step.
8. The video status switches to **Pending Approval **and the video player displays a message that the video has been submitted for approval along with the date and time it is submitted (viewed by clicking the **Submission Details** button).
9. You receive an email when an approver has approved or rejected your submission along with any comments that the approver includes. You also receive a [Notification](doc:notifications) flag. If your video is rejected, you may resubmit your video or delete it at that time.
