The **Preposition** tab option allows Account or Media Admins to immediately preposition important content. This means that a video is immediately pushed to VOD DMEs (once processing is through) instead of waiting for predefined times. This functionality must be enabled first before this option is available.

To preposition a video:

1. Navigate to the video that you want to push (preposition) immediately.

2. Click **Video Settings** button and then select the **Preposition** tab. (This setting may not be visible if your Account Admin has not [enabled](doc:allow-individual-video-prepositioning) this feature)

<Image alt="The Preposition setting has its own dropdown selection under Video Settings tab" align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/be31c21-advancedSettingsPrepositionVideo.png">
  The Preposition setting has its own dropdown selection under Video Settings tab
</Image>

3. Enter a **Start Date/Time** to preposition the video.

4. Enter a **Timezone**.

5. Click **Save**. The video will be pushed to all VOD DMEs on the date and time specified.

<Image title="prepositionVideo.png" alt={507} align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/33198a7-prepositionVideo.png">
  The video is pushed to all VOD DMEs on the start date and time you specify
</Image>
