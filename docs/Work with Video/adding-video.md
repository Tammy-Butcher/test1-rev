There are several methods you may use to add video content to your Rev portal. Use the **Upload Video** icon in the [Header navigation options](doc:the-rev-home-page#header-navigation-options) to upload, stream, and record videos, and add them to your Rev portal.  Adding video to the Rev portal requires you to be an [admin or contributor type role](doc:roles-and-permissions).

  Rev's Upload Icon
</Image>

> 👍 Tip
>
> The recommended [upload format](doc:supported-video-and-audio-formats) is **MP4** with an **H.264** video codec and **AAC** audio codec.

* Your most recent uploads remain visible in the **Upload Tray** dropdown. Click **See all uploads** to view them (or click the video title to go directly to the video). Click **Clear completed** to clear the recent uploads.
* Video uploads are automatically set to **Inactive**[status](doc:video-status) by default and must be manually activated. This allows you to control when videos are available for general viewing so that video settings such as thumbnails, categories, access controls, and so forth may be put in place first.
* You must select a valid [file type](doc:supported-video-and-audio-formats) or you will receive an error message.
* Once your video(s) finish uploading and transcoding, you receive an email with a link to the video. This means you do not have to keep manually checking your videos to see if they have finished processing.
* Use the [Video Settings](doc:update-basic-video-settings) button to modify metadata such as who may access the video, expiration dates, categories, and so forth.
* You may also [edit the video](doc:edit-a-video-clip) to add chapters and presentation slides if desired.
* You may also utilize H.265 / HEVC (High-Efficiency Video Coding).  (HEVC is covered by one or more claims of the HEVC patents listed at [https://patentlist.accessadvance.com](https://patentlist.accessadvance.com).)  H.265 will be converted to H.264 for use throughout our system.
