To update **Video Settings** in Rev:

1. Navigate to a video and hover over the **Video Settings** button in the top right corner.

2. Click **Details ** from the options that appear.  Several tabs appear that allow you to create and modify the video's metadata. Select a tab depending on which setting you want to update. 

3. This feature is an **Advanced Setting**.

Your Account Admin may configure Rev so that several different [transcoded instances](doc:manage-transcoding-settings) of your video are created when you upload or add videos. For example, you may have one that is **Standard Definition** while another is created at **High Definition 1080p**.

This is so that viewers may select the video quality that best fits the requirements of his/her computer or device.

Available instances of a video (if configured) are listed in the **Instances **section below the **Thumbnail** section.
