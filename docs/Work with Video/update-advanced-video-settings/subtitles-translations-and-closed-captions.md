To update **Video Settings** in Rev:

1. Navigate to a video and hover over the **Video Settings** button in the top right corner.

2. Click **Details ** from the options that appear.  Several tabs appear that allow you to create and modify the video's metadata. Select a tab depending on which setting you want to update. 

3. This feature is an **Advanced Setting**.

Subtitles and Closed Captions are both text versions of audio occurring in videos but are used for two different purposes.

**Subtitle (srt) **files are _not_ the same as enabling **Closed Captions (cc)** for a video. A subtitle file can be created in the language of your choice and you also have a great deal of control over formatting options. Subtitles are normally concerned with _only_ the _spoken_ words in a video and often used by people who can hear but who want or need to see the text version of the dialoge such as a person who needs the language translated.  Subtitles are managed on the [Languages](doc:video-languages) tab.

**Closed Captions**, on the other hand, are encrypted within the video file itself and you have less control over formatting. Further, closed captions normally also convey additional items other than the human voice such as sound effects that are occurring during the video (breaking glass, music playing, and so forth). Closed Captions are normally used by the deaf or hard of hearing so that the video can be better understood or to add context.

To enable closed captions for a video:

1. Click the **Advanced Settings** tab under **Video Settings**.

2. In the **Closed Captions** section, select the **Enabled **tab.

> 📘 Note
> 
> If the video includes **CEA-608** or **708** captions, you can enable the **Closed Captions** menu using this setting.

A **Closed Caption** link appears under the video **Audio** options so that users can select **Closed Captions** while viewing the video in the [video player](https://revdocs.vbrick.com/docs/rev-video-player-features).  Viewers must have a compatible browser that supports closed captioning otherwise it will not function even if enabled and present in a video.
