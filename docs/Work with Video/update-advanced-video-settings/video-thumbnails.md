To update **Video Settings** in Rev:

1. Navigate to a video and hover over the **Video Settings** button in the top right corner.

2. Click **Details** from the options that appear.  Several tabs appear that allow you to create and modify the video's metadata. Select a tab depending on which setting you want to update. 

3. This feature is an **Advanced Setting**.

## Set a Custom Thumbnail for a Video

**Thumbnails** provide a quick snapshot of what your video contains. When a video is uploaded, Rev automatically generates a thumbnail image for you. 

The auto-generated thumbnail is the default thumbnail for the video.  If a new thumbnail image is uploaded, the auto-generated thumbnail is discarded and may *not* be recovered.

### Upload a Thumbnail

Use the **Upload New** button or (**+**) icon in the middle of the default thumbnail image to upload an entirely new image from your computer to use a custom thumbnail for your video.

<Image alt="Click the (+) plus icon to upload a custom thumbnail for your video or click the Upload New button" align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/ad2af75-uploadThumbnail.png">
  Click the (+) plus icon to upload a custom thumbnail for your video or click the Upload New button
</Image>

> 👍 Tip
>
> View the [Supported File Types](doc:supported-file-types) topic for supported image types if you upload an image.  Keep in mind that the custom thumbnail should be ideally **16:9** aspect ratio and less than **2MB** in size for best results!  This will keep your thumbnail from stretching and promote quick load times.

### Pick a Thumbnail from the Video

You can also select a new thumbnail image from the video itself.  Click the **Choose from Video** button to select a frame from the video to use as the thumbnail.

<Image title="captureThumbnail.png" align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/a0e37ce-captureThumbnail.png" />

A miniature video player displays that allows you to play the video and capture the frame you want to use as your custom thumbnail image.   Make sure you **Save** your new setting before exiting the video.

> 🚧 Important!
>
> This feature is currently not available for Safari browsers.
>
> You may not choose a thumbnail from mp3, mp4, or 360 video formats at this time.
