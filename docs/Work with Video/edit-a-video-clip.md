Rev's video editor appears when **Edit Video** is clicked. The video player window shrinks and displays on the upper half of the screen while the thumbnail timeline appears below it.  Editing and interface buttons you will use to edit your video appear to the right of the editing interface. Each element of the video editor is described in the topics of this section.
