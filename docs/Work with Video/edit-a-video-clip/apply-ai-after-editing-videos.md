As a reminder, when you are in the editor and select **Save** or **Save As**, you create a new video version.  Depending on the scope of your changes and how much you have added or removed, the existing subtitles  may not be synchronized with the video.   Additionally, any Rev IQ generated metadata may not represent the newly edited video.  In order to help simplify the edit workflow, you may be prompted to re-apply subtitles or metadata.

As part of the **Save** or **Save As** workflow, if you have [Rev IQ Transcription and Translation](doc:rev-iq-transcription-and-translation) enabled and you are a **Rev IQ User**, you are prompted to either apply or reapply Rev IQ's AI metadata. This includes the ability to re-transcribe and regenerate the video metadata upon either saving or using the save as feature(s) in the Rev video editor.

It should be noted that these are optional selections and re-applications.  You do not need to re-transcribe, re-generate subtitles, or even re-generate the metadata.  This capability is provided here to simplify the workflow.  You may want to return for additional edits, and only re-generate once all your edits are complete (this would use fewer Rev IQ Credits, for example).

If you choose **Save**, you are notified that you will overwrite your original video with the edited version.

You are then provided the option(s) to transcribe the video through AI and to generate one or more of its metadata fields using Rev IQ.  [Rev AI features](doc:rev-ai) must be enabled by your Admin before this is available.

If you choose **Save As New**, you are notified that the video will be copied with the new features that you select.  Also, your Account Admin has the ability to automatically translate videos into specified languages on your Rev portal.  If this is the case, they are pre-selected for you as seen with the French and Spanish selections in the image seen below. This will be the case even if you choose save as for your edited video.

This means that you are not able to modify transcription options and may only decide what metadata options you want to regenerate for your video when you edit it.
