The video **Slice** and **Delete** icon(s) are used to create and delete video clips on a video's timeline.  Each slice you make, creates a new clip that you can then edit and manipulate to make your videos dynamic and engaging or to remove unnecessary material if needed by cutting it. 

For example, you can create a clip and then delete it from your video if it contains sensitive company data or information that is not relative to what you want to display in your overall content.

<Image align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/030ad76-videoSliceIcons.png" />

When you have finished editing the clips you have made through your slices, you can then save your video as an entirely new video that is different from the original you started with.  

Video clips that you create using the **Slice** icon can be edited or manipulated using a variety of methods. 

For example, you can:

* Move the clip to a different place in the video (by dragging the clip to a different spot on the timeline)
* Trim and adjust the clip's in and out points on the timeline
* Delete the clip from the video so it no longer appears

To create a video slice:

1. Click on the video timeline where you want to add your first slice.  Keep in mind that the first clip is *always* created from the *beginning* of the video to the location of the *first* slice you make with the **Slice** icon.

<Image alt="The first clip you create is made from the start of the video to the location of your first slice" align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/4cd6f44-sliceOne.png">
  The first clip you create is made from the start of the video to the location of your first slice
</Image>

2. All subsequent slices you add to the timeline continue to create clips relative to slices and to the left of clips already created.
3. For example, in the image below, three slices have been added to the video resulting in four clips (A, B, C, and D) that can now be further manipulated in the video.

<Image align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/4a4b65ff2a82b5f2059b88ec331ac022f487189dc7f10834e74667f6a6e5659a-sliceAndDeleteIcons.png" />

## Move a Video Clip on the Timeline

You can move video clips you have created with the **Slice** icon anywhere on the timeline by left clicking with mouse button and dragging the clip to its new location where you want it to appear when the video is played.

<Image alt="Click and drag the video clip to where you want it to appear on the timeline" align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/a2814f4-moveClip1.png">
  Click and drag the video clip to where you want it to appear on the timeline
</Image>

> 👍 Tip
>
> You may need to scale the timeline so you have a better view of the overall video by using the **Scale and Position** slider

1. Begin by clicking on the clip you want to move.  It will highlight in the primary color you have defined for your portal (in this example, blue).
2. Left-click and drag the clip to its new position on the timeline where you want it to appear.  Notice that as you drag the clip, a green highlight appears where you may drop it (between other clips that have been defined, for example).

<Image alt="As you are dragging the video clip, notice the green highlights that appear suggesting where it may be dropped" align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/b8466fe-moveClip2.png">
  As you are dragging the video clip, notice the green highlights that appear suggesting where it may be dropped
</Image>

3. The clip appears and plays in its new location once dropped.
4. The timeline will collapse and the blank space left in the clip's old position is automatically closed and filled by the video editor.
5. Make sure you continually play the video to test it and your new video is exactly how you want it to appear before you save it.

## Trim a Video Clip

When a video clip is created through your slices, "handles" appear on both the left and right side of the clip when you select it. These handles allow you to trim the video and adjust the clip's in and out points on the timeline by dragging them left or right as needed.

<Image alt="Use the &#x22;handles&#x22; on each side of the clip to trim or expand it as needed" align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/dd887f6-trimandexpand.png">
  Use the "handles" on each side of the clip to trim it as needed
</Image>

Drag the handles either left or right to trim the clip.  Keep in mind:

* You cannot expand the clip on the timeline beyond the original scope of the clip
* You can only trim the clip to the minimum size

## Delete a Video Clip

Use the **Delete** icon to remove a clip entirely from your video timeline.

<Image align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/86062a3-deleteClip.png" />

<br />

1. Highlight the clip you want to cut and hover over the **Delete** icon with your mouse.  
2. Notice that the clip border turns red.  Click the **Delete** icon to remove the clip.
3. Deletion is immediate and the timeline collapses over the region that has been cut (fills in).  Use the **Undo** icon if you need to return the clip to the timeline.
4. Again, make sure you test your video and play it before saving.  You may need to make some adjustments from your deletion first.

## Saving Video Edits

When you have finished editing your video, click the **Save** icon to the right of the player. You are given the option to either **Save** the edits you have made (replacing the original video) or you can select **Save As** to create a new video instead.

<Image alt="save icon" align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/379864cb8f1591f4e7d7638f8e5bb9469a68c43bcdb5250f2388db5261382251-saveIcon.png" />

* Use the **Reset** button at any time to undo all changes you have made and reset the video to its *original* state. *This will only work if you have not saved the video*.

<Image align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/a2ef15f348814e8b3481f9923d3b0130c730cc334ef3f90a0104de709c1ca73a-resetIcon.png" />

* Click the **Save** button (or **Save As New**) to save the video. *Note that this saves the video and replaces the original video.* If you want a copy of the original video, you should always use the **Download** icon first before you do any editing.
* The saved video will be retitled with the same name and the word "Edited" appended. Further, the **Description** of the edited video will note that the video has been edited. Metadata and analytics will remain unchanged.

> 🚧 Important!
>
> If the video and user previously required approval, the video must be submitted and go through the approval process again before it will be **Active**.
