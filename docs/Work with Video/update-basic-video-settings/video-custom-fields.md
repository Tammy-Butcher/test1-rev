To update **Video Settings** in Rev:

1. Navigate to a video and hover over the **Video Settings** button in the top right corner.

2. Click **Details ** from the options that appear.  Several tabs appear that allow you to create and modify the video's metadata. Select a tab depending on which setting you want to update. 

3. This feature is a **Basic Setting**.

Your Account Admin may have configured additional metadata fields for videos that are specific to your organization.

If custom fields have been set up by your Admin, they appear beneath the **Description **field. Just as with default field settings, required fields are marked by a red asterisk.

[Custom fields](doc:add-custom-fields#section-usage) are included in video searches and also appear on the **Video Basic Information** flyout on the video player (if configured to do so). Your Account Admin controls which fields are displayed and which are available only to users who can edit the video.
