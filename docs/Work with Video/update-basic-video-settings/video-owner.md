To update **Video Settings** in Rev:

1. Navigate to a video and hover over the **Video Settings** button in the top right corner.
2. Click **Details** from the options that appear.  Several tabs appear that allow you to create and modify the video's metadata. Select a tab depending on which setting you want to update. 
3. This feature is a **Basic Setting**.

<Image title="videoOwner.png" alt={334} align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/775504f-videoOwner.png">
  The uploader of a video is set to the Video Owner by default
</Image>

The **Video Owner** is, by default, the user who uploads the video. This information is shown throughout the Portal and Analytics. The Video Owner automatically has view and edit rights to a video.  This field can be reassigned in Video Settings. To assign a new Video Owner, click the **X** next to the current owner's name and type in the new owner's name.

<Image title="assignVideoOwner.png" alt={559} align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/7b700fd-assignVideoOwner.png">
  Assign a new Video Owner by clicking the X next to the current owner's name
</Image>

* Video ownership is transferred when you assign a new **Video Owner** in this field, including view and edit rights unless otherwise specified in Access Controls
* When re-assigning a video asset, the new owner is notified
* Video ownership can include the **Media Viewer** role
* The uploader does *not* retain view and/or edit rights when the Video Owner field is modified unless they are granted **Edit Access** in **Video Access Controls**
* Admins and anyone with edit rights can modify the Video Owner field

<Image title="videoOwnerEditAccess.png" alt={502} align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/358e8da-videoOwnerEditAccess.png">
  To provide additional edit access to users other than the Video Owner, include them in the Access Control List
</Image>
