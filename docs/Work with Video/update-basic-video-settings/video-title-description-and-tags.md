To update **Video Settings** in Rev:

1. Navigate to a video and hover over the **Video Settings** button in the top right corner.

2. Click **Details ** from the options that appear.  Several tabs appear that allow you to create and modify the video's metadata. Select a tab depending on which setting you want to update.

3. This feature is a **Basic Setting**.

Edit the **Title **,**Description**, and **Tags** fields as needed (a title is required).  You may use Rev's text editor controls to customize the text including font size, color, formatting, and the ability to open a hyperlink and insert emojis into the description if desired. This formatting is then displayed on the [Video Basic Information](doc:rev-video-player-features#video-basic-information) panel flyout on the video player.

To add a tag, begin typing the text and then press **Enter **to save it in the **Tags **control box. 

- Multiple tags may be entered
- Spaces may be used
- Tags are not case sensitive
- Click the **X** next to the tag to delete the tag from the video

You can also choose to add chapters to your video by clicking the **Edit** link which will take you to the Rev [Video Editor](doc:add-chapters-to-a-video). When you add chaptering to a video, it is similar to adding bookmarks for your viewers that allows them to jump directly to content that may be relative or of specific interest to them.

If you have [Rev IQ credits](doc:rev-license-types-and-add-ons#rev-iq-credits) and the video has an [English transcript](doc:rev-iq-transcription-and-translation#automatic-and-default-transcription-settings) you can use Vbrick's generative AI feature to summarize its transcript and automatically generate a video's metadata.  

This feature must also be enabled first by an Account Admin in **Media Settings** under the [Generative AI Tools](doc:generative-ai-tools) section.

The AI generated **description** will be added to any existing text in the description field and is always appended with the line " **_ Automatically Generated Description _** " at the start of the description text.  AI generated **tags** are also added to any existing tags you already have added to your video.  

However, the following metadata is _replaced_ when you use the **Generate** links for a video:

- Title
- Chapters (chapter images are also automatically generated from the first frame of the chapter and can either be replaced or deleted as needed in the [video editor](doc:add-chapters-to-a-video))

The generated text may always be edited as needed.

You are notified through email and through the Rev [Notifications](doc:notifications) feature when the fields have finished generating. Keep in mind that you should _always_ verify this information through trusted sources!

> 📘 Note
> 
> Remember, if there is not an **English** transcript available for the video, you will not be able to use this feature even if it has been enabled!

It is important to keep in mind that AI uses your _transcript_ to generate this metadata for the video.  This means that if the transcript of the video does not match what you intend to convey with the video's _content_, you need to be aware of this; in other words, context is important!  

Remember this when using any generative AI feature as you may not receive what is intended or you may receive "lacking context" messages.
