If [custom fields](doc:video-custom-fields) have been configured by your Account Admin, their values are also able to be edited in the [Bulk Edit](doc:bulk-edit-video-settings) interface.  Custom fields are listed at the very bottom of the bulk edit interface.

<Image alt="All custom fields are located at the bottom of Rev's native fields" align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/7047b93-bulkEditCustomFields.png">
  All custom fields are located at the bottom of Rev's native fields
</Image>

The **Replace** and **Remove** functions may be used with custom fields.  

> 📘 Note
>
> You may not remove a custom field that has been deemed a required field.
