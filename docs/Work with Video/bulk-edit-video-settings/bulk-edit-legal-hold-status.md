Use the [Legal Hold](doc:the-legal-hold-setting) icon in the **Bulk Edit** icon controls to apply or remove a legal hold on several videos at once.  

The icon is a toggle function.  This means that to apply a legal hold, *only* those videos that are *not* in legal hold status may be selected. To remove a legal hold, *only* those videos *in* legal hold status may be selected.  

If you select both, the icon will not be visible since it will not function correctly.

<Image title="legalHoldLock.png" align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/8daf5b3-legalHoldIcon.png" />

After you select your videos, there are two steps to applying or removing a Legal Hold:

1. Click the **Legal Hold Lock** icon in the Bulk Edit controls.

2. Click the **Apply Legal Hold** or **Remove Legal Hold** message box that appears to the left of the lock (whichever the case may be).

<Image title="applyLegalHold.png" alt="Click the Legal Hold Lock icon > Apply Legal Hold message to bulk edit legal hold settings on selected videos" align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/bff418d-legalHoldBulkEdit.png">
  Click the Legal Hold Lock icon > Apply Legal Hold message to bulk edit legal hold settings on selected videos
</Image>

3. You will be asked to confirm your selection before saving.
