Use the [Ratings](doc:video-features#enable-comments-ratings-and-downloading) control in the [Bulk Edit](doc:bulk-edit-video-settings) interface to edit the ratings video setting on several videos at once. If the **Enable Ratings** checkbox is selected, the video may be rated by the viewer.  This control enables or disables this ability.
