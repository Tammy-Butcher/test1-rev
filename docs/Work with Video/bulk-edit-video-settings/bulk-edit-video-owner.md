To assign a new [Video Owner](doc:video-owner) to several videos at once, type the new owner in the **Video Owner** field in the [Bulk Editing](doc:bulk-edit-video-settings) interface.  As soon as you begin typing, a list appears to choose from that matches the values you enter.  The new owner is assigned to the videos you select upon saving.
