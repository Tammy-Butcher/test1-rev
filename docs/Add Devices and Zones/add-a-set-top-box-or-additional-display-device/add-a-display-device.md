The **Devices** > **Display** module is used to view, activate, and inactivate additional **Devices** and **Integrations** that are linked to Rev.

## Activate a Display Device

Display devices, regardless integration, are in either **Active** or **Inactive** status once registered with Rev. Only those that have been moved to the **Active Displays** tab are able to communicate and work with Rev. 

<Image title="displayDeviceModule.png" alt={807} align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/eee589e-displayDeviceModule.png">
  All additional Display Integrations are activated or inactivated from the Devices > Display module
</Image>

* The **Inactive Display** tab lists all devices that are registered in Rev but not activated. These devices cannot receive streams or connect with Rev until they are activated. 

* The **Active Displays** tab contains all devices that are activated.

* Click **Actions** > **Edit** next to the device you want to activate or inactivate. An edit screen appears where you can change the device status. You can also use the **Bulk Actions** dropdown to change the status of several devices at once if necessary.

### Rev for Apple TV

**Rev for Apple TV** is an Apple TV OS application that provides the ability to view Live (Public or All Users) Rev Webcasts on Apple TVs. The application is available on the Apple TV Store and is free to download.   Rev for Apple TV is a supported integration and appears in the **Display** module for activation once installed.

For downloading, installation and Rev registration, refer to the [Rev for Apple TV Integration Guide](https://portal.vbrick.com/doc/Integrations/AppleTV/Vbrick%20Rev%20for%20Apple%20TV%20Integration%20Guide.pdf).

## Edit a Display

The status of a **Display** device (e.g., a Rev for Apple TV app seen below) as well as some metadata details may be edited within Rev. The Display device may also provide the ability to locally (to the device) change configuration.

From the **Active Displays** or **Inactive Displays** tabs, Account Admins can click on the Device Name to view the edit page. Within the Edit page, you can change configurations and information. Some information cannot be changed, however.

<Image title="editDisplayDevice.png" alt={607} align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/d343d08-editDisplayDevice.png">
  Only the Name and Unique ID of the Display Device may not be edited
</Image>
